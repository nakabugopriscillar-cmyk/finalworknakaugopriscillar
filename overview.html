BALANCE FOR ALL

Domestic violence is a serious issue that affects millions of people worldwide. Our organization is dedicated to providing support, resources, and empowerment to survivors of domestic violence.

50% of women aged 15-49 have experienced intimate partner violenceand gender-based violence at least once in their lifetime.

14,073 cases of domestic violence were reported to the police in 2024, marking a 4% decrease from 14,681 cases in 2023.

This is to act as the voice to the voiceless ,light in the darkness for the suffered victims of domestic violence and thus bring equality to our community.

This project will be the platform to report any forms of violence.
