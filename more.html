WHAT IS DOMESTIC VIOLENCE?

This refers to abusive behaviour used by one individual to establish power and control over the intimate individual. These may include any behaviour that intimidates,manipulate,humiliate,isolate,threaten,blame,hurt,injure someone.

Types Of Domestic violence

  • Physcial abuse
    Sexual abuse
    Emotional abuse
    Psychological abuse

    • Forms of domestic violence.

    Physical abuse;Physical violence, such as hitting, pushing, kicking, or restraining.

    Emotional abuse: Verbal or non-verbal behavior that belittles, humiliates, or controls, such as name-calling, insults, or threats.

    Sexual abuse: Forced or coercive sex, including rape, unwanted touching, or sexual exploitation.

    Economic abuse: Controlling or withholding financial resources, such as money, employment, or education.

    Psychological abuse: Manipulating or controlling behavior, such as gaslighting, isolation, or intimidation.

    Digital abuse: Using technology to control, harass, or intimidate, such as tracking, monitoring, or threatening online.

    Verbal abuse: Verbal aggression, such as yelling, swearing, or name-calling.

    Impact of Domestic violence

    Physical harm: Domestic violence can result in serious physical injuries, including bruises, broken bones, and even death.

    Emotional trauma: Domestic violence can cause long-term emotional trauma, including anxiety, depression, and PTSD.

    Economic instability: Domestic violence can lead to financial instability, including loss of income, debt, and poverty.

    Impact on children: Children who witness domestic violence can experience emotional and psychological trauma, affecting their development and well-being.

    Signs Of Domestic Violence

    Physical signs: Unexplained injuries, bruises, or broken bones.

    Emotional signs: Anxiety, depression, fear, or anxiety around a partner.

    Behavioral signs: Isolation from friends and family, controlling behavior, or threats.
