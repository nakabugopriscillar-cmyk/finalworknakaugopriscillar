HOW TO STOP THIS VIOLENCE

Domestic violence is a pervasive and complex issue that affects individuals, families, and communities. Breaking the cycle of violence requires a collective effort and a multifaceted approach.

As An Individual

  • Be an active bystander.

  • In cases you witness or suspect, directly safely intervene or call out.

  • Support survivors with empathy.

  • Challenge the culture of violence.

  • Self-education: Learn about domestic violence, its signs, and its consequences.

  • Healthy relationships: Practice healthy communication, boundaries, and conflict resolution.

  • Support networks: Build a support network of friends, family, and professionals.

  • Empowerment: Focus on self-care, self-love, and personal growth.

  • Seek help: Reach out to hotlines, counseling services, or trusted individuals if experiencing abuse.

As Family

  • Communication: Model healthy communication and conflict resolution skills.

  • Support survivors: Believe and support family members who disclose domestic violence.

  • Safe environment: Create a safe and supportive environment for survivors.

  • Resources: Provide resources and information about domestic violence services.

  • Non-violent modeling: Model non-violent behavior and respect in relationships.

  • Provide a safe place

  • Support children

  • Establish healthy relationships

AS Community

  • Provide community education about violence.

  • Moblization.

  • Ensure coordinated response.

  • Increased funding for survivor services.

  • Establishing policies against domestic violence.

  • Awareness campaigns: Organize awareness campaigns, events, and fundraisers to support survivors.

  • Support local organizations: Partner with local organizations providing domestic violence services.

  • Training and education: Provide training for professionals, such as law enforcement and healthcare providers.

  • Community engagement: Engage men and boys in prevention efforts and promote positive masculinity.

  • Safe spaces: Create safe spaces for survivors to share their stories and receive support.
